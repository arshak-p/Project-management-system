# 🦜 Colour Parrot — How to Use the System
### A Simple, Step-by-Step Guide for Everyone

> **Read this once and you will know how to do everything in the system.**
> Written in simple English. No technical knowledge needed.

---

# PART 1 — Getting Started (Everyone Reads This)

---

## ✅ Step 1 — How to Log In

1. Open your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari)
2. Go to the Colour Parrot system website
3. You will see the login page
4. Type your **email address** and **password**
5. Click **Sign In**
6. You are in! ✅

> 💡 **If you forgot your password** — contact your Admin or Agency Manager to reset it for you.

---

## ✅ Step 2 — What You See After Logging In

After logging in, you will see **two main things:**

**1. The Sidebar (left side)**
- This is the menu
- It shows only the pages you are allowed to see
- Click any item in the sidebar to go to that page

**2. The Main Area (right side)**
- This is where the content shows up
- It changes depending on which page you clicked

**Top right corner has:**
- 🌙 **Moon/Sun icon** = switch between dark and light mode
- 🔔 **Bell icon** = your notifications
- **Your name/initials** = click to go to your profile

---

## ✅ Step 3 — Changing Between Dark and Light Mode

- Click the **moon icon** (top right) to switch to light mode
- Click the **sun icon** to switch back to dark mode
- The system saves your preference

---

## ✅ Step 4 — Updating Your Profile

1. Click your **initials or name** (top right corner)
2. You will go to your **Profile page**
3. Here you can update:
   - Your first and last name
   - Your phone number
   - Your date of birth
   - Your profile photo
4. Click **Save** when done

---

# PART 2 — Daily Use for Each Role

---

# 👤 IF YOU ARE A SPECIALIST / CREATOR

> Your job: Do the tasks assigned to you and update their status.

---

## Your Main Page — "My Workspace"

When you log in, click **My Workspace** from the sidebar.

This is where you will spend most of your time. It shows only **your tasks**.

---

### The 6 Tabs at the Top

You will see 6 tabs at the top of My Workspace. Here is what each one does:

---

**Tab 1 — Today's Task** *(this is the first tab you see)*

- Shows ALL your active tasks (everything you need to work on)
- This is your **daily to-do list**
- Tasks are sorted by priority — most urgent ones appear first
- **Use this tab every morning** to see what you need to do today

---

**Tab 2 — Start Date**

- Shows your tasks sorted by when they were assigned/started
- Useful to see which tasks you have been working on the longest
- Good to check if an old task has been sitting too long

---

**Tab 3 — Due Date**

- Shows tasks that are coming close to their due date
- **Check this tab in the afternoon** to make sure nothing is being missed
- Tasks highlighted in yellow or red need urgent attention

---

**Tab 4 — Deadline**

- Shows tasks sorted by their **final hard deadline**
- A deadline is stricter than a due date — it is the absolute last day
- **Check this tab every Monday** to plan your week

---

**Tab 5 — Completed**

- Shows all tasks you have finished
- Good for tracking your own progress
- Your Team Head can also see this

---

**Tab 6 — All Tasks**

- Shows every task assigned to you
- Sorted by priority and date
- Use this if you want to see the full picture

---

Each task card has a glowing border and shadow that shows its active status:

| Glow Color | What It Means | What You Should Do |
|------------|--------------|-------------------|
| 🟢 **Emerald Green** | Client Approved / Completed-Launched | Task is verified finished |
| 🔵 **Blue** | In-House Approved / Client Review / Pending Review | Task is actively under review |
| 🔴 **Red (Pulsing)** | Rework / Revision / Re-edit or Urgent / Overdue | Corrections needed or critical task — prioritize this |
| 🟡 **Amber** | High Priority task | Work on this next |
| 🟢 **Primary Green** | Actively In Progress | Keep working on it |
| ⚪ **Slate Grey** | Default / Low/Medium Priority | Standard queue item |

**Date Badge Colors:**
- 📅 **Posted Date:** Sky Blue badge (`POSTED: YYYY-MM-DD`).
- 🟢 **Start Date:** Emerald Green badge (`START: YYYY-MM-DD`).
- 🟡 **Due Date:** Amber Yellow badge (`DUE: YYYY-MM-DD`).
- 🔴 **Deadline:** Bold Red badge (`DEADLINE: YYYY-MM-DD`) with pulse animation.

---

### How to Open a Task

1. Click anywhere on the task card
2. A **Task Detail panel** will open
3. Here you can see everything about the task:
   - The task name and description
   - Which project it belongs to
   - Who assigned it to you
   - The due date and deadline
   - Files attached (briefs, references)
   - Comments from your Team Head or PM

---

### How to Update a Task Status

When you are working on a task, you need to update its status to let your Team Head know where you are.

1. Click on the task to open it
2. Find the **Status / State** field
3. Click the dropdown
4. Choose the correct status:
   - **In Progress** = you are actively working on it
   - **Team Head Review** = you are done, send it to your Team Head to check
5. Click **Save**

> 🚨 **Important:** When you finish a task, ALWAYS change it to **"Team Head Review"**. This tells your Team Head to look at your work. If you don't change it, they won't know you are done.

---

### How to Add a Comment on a Task

Comments are how you communicate about a task. Use them to:
- Ask questions about the task
- Tell your Team Head you finished something
- Explain what you did or any problems you faced

**Steps:**
1. Open the task (click on it)
2. Scroll down to the **Comments section**
3. Click the comment box
4. Type your message
5. Press **Send / Submit**

> 💡 Your Team Head and Project Manager will get a notification when you comment.

---

### How to Upload a File to a Task

When you finish a design, video, or document, upload it directly to the task:

1. Open the task
2. Find the **Attachments section**
3. Click **Upload** or drag and drop your file
4. The file is now attached to the task for everyone to see

---

### Your Daily Routine as a Specialist

**Every morning:**
1. Log in
2. Go to **My Workspace → Today's Task**
3. Look at your tasks sorted by priority
4. Start with the most urgent ones (red or yellow borders first)

**During the day:**
1. Work on your tasks
2. When done with a task → change status to **Team Head Review**
3. Upload your finished work to the task
4. Add a comment saying "Completed — please review"

**Every evening:**
1. Go to **Due Date** tab
2. Check if anything is due soon
3. Plan what to work on tomorrow

---

# 👤 IF YOU ARE A TEAM HEAD

> Your job: Manage your team's tasks. Review their work. Approve or send back for changes. Also do your own tasks.

---

## Your Main Page — The Dashboard (Lead Overview)

When you log in, you see the **Lead Overview** dashboard with **two columns:**

---

### Left Column — "My Personal Missions"

- Shows tasks that are **assigned directly to YOU**
- You also get tasks just like a Specialist
- Click any task to open and work on it
- Update the status the same way a Specialist does (see above)

---

### Right Column — "Team Operations / Review Pipeline"

- Shows tasks from **your team members** that are at the **"Team Head Review"** stage
- These are tasks your specialists have finished and sent to you for approval

---

### How to Approve a Task

When a team member finishes their work and sends it for review:

1. Look at the task in the **right column**
2. Click the task to open it and check the work
3. If the work is good, click the **✅ Approve** button
4. The task automatically moves to the next stage (e.g., Client Review)
5. Done!

---

### How to Reject a Task (Send Back for Changes)

1. Click the task to open it
2. If the work needs changes, click the **❌ Reject** button
3. The task goes back to the Specialist
4. **Important:** Add a comment explaining WHY you rejected it and WHAT needs to change:
   - Go to the Comments section
   - Write clearly what needs to be fixed
   - Click Send

> 💡 Always write a rejection comment. Otherwise your Specialist won't know what to fix.

---

### My Workspace Tab

You also have a **My Workspace** tab in your sidebar. It works exactly like a Specialist's My Workspace. Use it to manage your own personal tasks with the 6 filter tabs (Today's Task, Start Date, Due Date, Deadline, Completed, All Tasks).

---

### Your Daily Routine as a Team Head

**Every morning:**
1. Log in → see the **Lead Overview dashboard**
2. Check the **right column** — are there tasks waiting for your approval?
3. Review them and Approve or Reject
4. Check your **left column** — do you have personal tasks to work on?

**During the day:**
1. Review work as your team submits it
2. Give feedback through comments
3. Approve good work, reject work that needs improvement

**Every week:**
1. Go to **Team Intelligence** to check team performance
2. Go to **Tasks** to spot any overdue or stuck tasks
3. Check **Calendar** to see what deadlines are coming

---

# 👤 IF YOU ARE A PROJECT MANAGER (PM)

> Your job: Create projects and tasks. Assign work to the team. Monitor progress. Ensure deadlines are met.

---

## How to Create a New Project

1. Click **Projects** in the sidebar
2. Click the **+ New Project** button (top right)
3. Fill in the details:
   - **Project Name** (e.g., "Healthy Cart — June Campaign")
   - **Client name**
   - **Start date**
   - **End date / Deadline**
4. Click **Save**
5. The project is now created ✅

---

## How to Create a Task

1. Click **Tasks** in the sidebar
2. Click **+ New Task**
3. Fill in:
   - **Task Title** (e.g., "Workers Day Poster")
   - **Project** — which project does this belong to
   - **Module** (e.g., Poster, Video, Copy)
   - **Assignee** — choose the team member from the dropdown
   - **Priority** — Urgent / High / Medium / Low
   - **Posting Date** (start date)
   - **Due Date** (when it should be done)
   - **Deadline** (absolute last day)
   - **Description/Brief** — write what needs to be done clearly
4. Click **Save** ✅

> 💡 The Specialist will immediately see this task in their **My Workspace**.

---

## How to Assign a Task to Someone

When creating or editing a task:
1. Find the **Assignee** field
2. Click the dropdown
3. Search or scroll to find the team member
4. Select their name
5. Save

---

## How to Check Task Progress

**Option 1 — Tasks Page:**
1. Click **Tasks** in the sidebar
2. See all tasks with their current status
3. Filter by project, assignee, or status

**Option 2 — Kanban Board:**
1. Click **Kanban Board**
2. See all tasks in columns by status
3. Tasks stuck in one column for too long need attention

**Option 3 — Calendar:**
1. Click **Calendar**
2. See what is due on each day
3. Spot deadline problems in advance

---

## How to Archive a Project (When It's Done)

1. Go to **Projects**
2. Find the project
3. Click the **Archive** button
4. Confirm
5. The project moves to archived (all data is kept safely)

To see archived projects: turn on **"Show Archived"** toggle on the Projects page.

---

## How to Use Cycles / Sprints

Cycles are weekly or bi-weekly work windows — a focused set of tasks for a time period.

1. Click **Cycles** in the sidebar
2. Click **+ New Cycle**
3. Give it a name (e.g., "Week 22 Sprint")
4. Set start and end dates
5. Add tasks to this cycle
6. Track progress within that time window

---

## How to Use the Activity Log

1. Click **Activity Log** in the sidebar
2. See every action taken by every person in the system
3. Use the search bar to filter by:
   - A person's email
   - An action type (e.g., "created", "updated", "deactivated")
   - A task or project name

---

## Your Daily Routine as a PM

**Every morning:**
1. Check **Dashboard** → see overall agency health
2. Check **Tasks** → look for overdue or red-border tasks
3. Check **Calendar** → see deadlines this week

**During the week:**
1. Create new tasks as new work comes in
2. Reassign tasks if someone is overloaded
3. Communicate through task comments

**Every Friday:**
1. Review **Team Intelligence** → check performance
2. Review **Timesheets** → check time spent
3. Review **Cycles** → close completed sprints, start new ones

---

# 👤 IF YOU ARE AN ADMIN / AGENCY MANAGER

> You have access to everything. In addition to all PM features, you also manage the system itself.

---

## How to Add a New Team Member

1. Click **Team** in the sidebar
2. Click **+ Invite / Add Member**
3. Fill in:
   - Full name
   - Email address
   - Role (Admin, PM, Team Head, Specialist, HR, Strategist)
   - Job title
4. Click **Save / Send Invite**
5. They receive an email with login instructions

---

## How to Change Someone's Role

1. Click **Team**
2. Find the team member
3. Click on their profile
4. Change the **Role** dropdown
5. Save

> ⚠️ Be careful — changing a role changes what they can see and do in the system.

---

## How to Deactivate a Team Member Who Left

1. Click **Team**
2. Find the person
3. Click **Deactivate**
4. They can no longer log in
5. All their past work and tasks are preserved

---

## How to Set Up the Workflow (Task Stages)

The Workflow defines the journey a task takes from start to finish.

1. Click **Workflow** in the sidebar
2. See the list of current stages
3. To add a new stage: click **+ Add Stage**, type the name, save
4. To reorder: drag stages into the correct order

> ⚠️ Only change the workflow when you need a fundamentally new stage. This affects all tasks.

---

## How to Use the Strategist View (Executive Report)

1. Click **Strategist** in the sidebar
2. See the **Lifecycle & Tracking Ledger** — a full table of every task with:
   - Task code, specialist name, module
   - When it was assigned, started, completed
   - Target/due date (color coded: green = on time, red = delayed)
   - Number of times reworked

**Use this for:**
- Client reports
- Identifying quality issues (high rework count)
- Presenting delivery timelines

---

## How to Create a Database Backup

1. Click **Backups** in the sidebar
2. Click **Create Backup**
3. Wait for it to complete
4. Download the backup file for safe storage

> 💡 Create a backup before any major changes to the system.

---

# PART 3 — Notifications (Everyone)

---

## How to Read Your Notifications

Click the 🔔 **bell icon** (top right corner).

You get a notification when:
- A task is assigned to you
- Someone comments on your task
- Your task is approved or rejected
- Your task status changes

---

## How to Clear Notifications

**Mark one as read:** Click on the notification — it opens the related task and marks it read.

**Mark all as read:** Click the **"Mark all as read"** button at the top.

---

## How to Enable Desktop Notifications

Get browser pop-ups even when you are on another tab:

1. Open Notifications page
2. Click **"Enable Desktop Alerts"**
3. Click **Allow** on the browser prompt
4. Done — you will now get desktop pop-ups ✅

# PART 4 — Quick Reference

---

## What to Do in Common Situations

---

**"I finished my task — what do I do?"**
1. Upload your finished file to the task
2. Change status to **"Team Head Review"**
3. Comment: "Completed. Please review."

---

**"My task was rejected — what do I do?"**
1. Read the Team Head's comment to understand what to fix
2. Make the changes
3. Change status back to **"Team Head Review"**
4. Comment: "Fixed as requested — please review again."

---

**"I need to ask a question about my task"**
1. Open the task
2. Go to Comments
3. Type your question
4. Click Send — the Team Head and PM will be notified

---

**"A task is taking longer than expected"**
1. Open the task
2. Add a comment: "This is taking longer than expected. Estimated finish: [date]."
3. Your Team Head and PM will be notified and can adjust the deadline

---

**"I want to find a specific task quickly"**
1. Click **Tasks** in the sidebar
2. Use the search bar or filter dropdowns
3. Filter by project, assignee, status, or priority

## Tips for Success

**Specialists:**
- ✅ Check My Workspace every morning
- ✅ Always change status when you finish work
- ✅ Write a comment when submitting for review
- ❌ Never leave a task on "In Progress" when you are actually done

**Team Heads:**
- ✅ Check the right column of Dashboard every morning
- ✅ Always write a rejection reason
- ✅ Review Team Intelligence weekly
- ❌ Never approve without checking the work

**Project Managers:**
- ✅ Always set both a Due Date AND a Deadline on tasks
- ✅ Write a clear brief in the task description
- ✅ Check Kanban Board daily for stuck tasks
- ✅ Use Activity Log to investigate issues

**Admins:**
- ✅ Create a backup before major changes
- ✅ Deactivate team members immediately when they leave
- ✅ Keep the Workflow stages simple
- ✅ Use Agency Roadmap for capacity planning
